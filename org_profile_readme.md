## 🔬 About Hoosha AI

**Hoosha AI** is an independent AI research lab and systems engineering team dedicated to advancing continuous-time generative modeling, differential attention mechanisms, and scalable GPU compute infrastructure.

### 🌟 Key Research Focus Areas

- 🌊 **Continuous Flow Matching (CFM)**: Simulation-free velocity vector field alignment for rapid ODE trajectories.
- ⚡ **Differential Attention**: High-precision attention architectures for long-context reasoning.
- 🧠 **LLM Epistemic Boundaries**: Probing internal logits, entropy levels, and RL alignment (GRPO).
- 🚀 **CUDA & Triton Kernels**: High-performance fused operators for modern AI hardware.
