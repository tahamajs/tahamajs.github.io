Mathematical Research Pipelines

Mathematical formulations driving our generative AI & post-training RL engines.

Flow Matching ODE

Continuous Velocity Fields

Continuous probability paths transforming noise $x_0 \sim p_0$ to target distributions $x_1 \sim p_1$ via linear vector fields $v_t(x_t) = x_1 - x_0$.

$$ \frac{dx_t}{dt} = v_t(x_t), \quad x_t = t x_1 + (1-t) x_0 $$
Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)

4B LLM Mathematical Reasoning

Policy optimization over output candidate groups $\{q_i\}_{i=1}^G$ without requiring a dedicated critic network, maximizing normalized advantage scores.

$$ \mathcal{J}_{\text{GRPO}}(\theta) = \mathbb{E}\left[ \sum_{i=1}^G \min\left( \frac{\pi_\theta(q_i)}{\pi_{\text{old}}(q_i)} \tilde{A}_i, \text{clip}(\dots)\tilde{A}_i \right) \right] $$
